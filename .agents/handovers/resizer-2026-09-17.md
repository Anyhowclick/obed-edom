## OPEN 1 (PRIORITY) — high offline-write fallback rate

- **SYMPTOM:** 704 of 3290 offline-write specs (~21%) fell to the **AppleScript fallback** instead
  of the surgical offline patcher; **126 of 148 slides** had `missed>0`. Worst by missed: slide 123
  (applied 158 / missed 90), 57 (missed 27), 70 (24), 103 (19), 125 (17), 135 (17).
  `value_clean=True` throughout — correctness is fine; the concern is speed + Keynote dependence
  (the fallback opens a Keynote AppleScript session).
- **EXPECTED:** the surgical IWA offline write should handle most specs; ~21% fallback suggests a
  systematic gap — some spec kind / owning-member case the patcher refuses and defers. Closing it
  would cut wall-time and reduce Keynote reliance (the whole point of the offline writer).
- **WHERE:** `full_run.log` lines `Offline-write slide N: applied=A missed=M`. Logic in
  `src/obed_edom/offline_write.py` (`_fallback_specs_by_slide`, `patch_results`, the offline patch
  path). Start by characterizing WHICH specs miss (kind/member/geometry field) on the worst slides
  (123, 57, 70), then decide whether the patcher can be extended to own them surgically.
