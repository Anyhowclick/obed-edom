## OPEN 1 — offline-write fallback rate (CHARACTERIZED 2026-09-17; hypothesis overturned)

- **ORIGINAL SYMPTOM:** 704 of 3290 offline-write specs (~21%) fell to the AppleScript fallback;
  126 of 148 slides had `missed>0` (`value_clean=True` throughout — correctness fine; the concern
  was speed + Keynote dependence). The original hypothesis was a *systematic patcher gap* — some
  spec kind the surgical writer refuses and defers — worth extending the patcher to own.
- **FINDING (the hypothesis is WRONG).** Instrumented the patcher to tag every deferred spec with a
  coarse miss reason (`feat/offline-fallback-reasons` @ `5ea2ce2`, per-spec `miss_reasons` aligned
  with `missed_specs` → `run_offline_write` `fallbackReasons`/`fallbackReasonsBySlide` histogram),
  then re-ran the full deck (2026-09-17; reproduced the benchmark exactly — 3822 applied / missed 0,
  zorderGui 0, fallback covered every spec, no unwritable). The 704 misses break down as:
  - **`text-autosize` 427 (61%)** — Keynote's autosize render cache; only a live write refreshes
    `naturalSize`. Fundamental.
  - **`masked-media` 188 (27%)** — a crop cannot be proven offline; the writer refuses rather than
    mis-write. Fundamental.
  - **`group-needs-keynote:group-residual` 83 (12%)** — residual/rotated groups need live layout.
  - **`group-child-scale` 6 (0.9%)** — the only arguably-closeable family.
  - **`unresolved` = 0** and **`*-resize-unwritable` = 0** — the two "maybe closeable" families
    (addressing/reconcile gaps, editableBezier node rescaling) contributed NOTHING. There is no
    surgical-patcher gap.
  - Worst slides confirm the mix: 123 `{text-autosize 86, masked-media 4}`, 57 `{ta 26, mm 1}`,
    70 `{ta 23, mm 1}`, 103 `{ta 19}`, 135 `{mm 17}`, card-wall 125/127 carry the group-residual +
    child-scale specs.
- **CONCLUSION:** ~99% of the fallback is three *fundamental* classes the offline writer correctly
  defers because they are not offline-recoverable (autosize text, media crops, residual groups).
  Extending the patcher to "own" them would mean writing geometry it cannot prove. The one closeable
  family (`group-child-scale`, 6 specs) is not worth the risk. The fallback is doing what it should;
  the ~21% is not a gap to close.
- **RE-SCOPED → research (owner, 2026-09-17):** the real lever on wall-time / Keynote-dependence is
  the two dominant fundamental classes, revisited as offline-recoverability research now that there
  is capacity: **`text-autosize` (61%)** — is autosize `naturalSize` derivable offline (a live-seed
  refresh path; the `reported`/bulk-geometry soft-seed exists for text *position* but not autosize
  *size*)? — and **`masked-media` (27%)** — can a real crop be recovered/written offline? Neither is
  a patcher extension; both are their own investigation. Two Opus researchers each spun up per issue.
- **EVIDENCE:** log `output/bank/2026-09-17/fallback-reasons-run/reasons_run.log` (the `Offline-write
  fallback reasons:` + `worst slides` lines); output deck `~/Desktop/Full_Report_Card_reasons_CG.key`.
  Instrumentation is standing diagnostics — keep it; every future run now emits the histogram.
  Logic lives in `iwa_write._slide_edits` (the `_miss(reason)` tags) and
  `offline_write._fallback_reason_histogram`.
